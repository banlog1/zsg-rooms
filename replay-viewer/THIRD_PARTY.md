# Temple Prediction

The temple decorator seed sequence follows the MIT-licensed published ZSG
`src/filter_common.zig` at revision 073e1d1f3150213c7c3787eda7ef8106cb789817,
also used in this repository's `tools/filter-worker/model_loot.h`.
https://github.com/DuncanRuns/ZigSeedGlitchless

components are bundled. Loot evaluation calls the installed Minecraft runtime.
