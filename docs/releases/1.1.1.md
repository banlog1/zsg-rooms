## What's New

**ZSG Rooms 1.1.1 + Replay Viewer 0.6.1**

- **Easy installation:** Set up the complete racing and replay suite through
  MultiMC using the import link above. A matching `.mrpack` is also available
  for other compatible launchers.
- **Independent camera sampling:** Standard, 60 Hz, 120 Hz and advanced 240 Hz
  options work with every recording preset. Higher rates capture smoother
  first-person look angles, limited by the recording player's rendered FPS.
- **Super Detailed recording:** An optional level above Detailed captures
  additional first-person presentation and original local-player sounds for
  Faithful Follow. Camera rate and Performance mode remain separate choices.
- **Capture health display:** An optional indicator shows measured camera
  sampling, dropped camera samples and recorder queue pressure.
- **Viewer/recorder performance:** Reduced redundant detailed-inventory work
  and trail-index reads while preserving their recorded information.
- **Sound fixes:** Improved mining-sound capture and prevented queued local
  sounds from playing when skipping through a replay.

Standard detail and Standard camera sampling remain the defaults. Higher
quality costs additional recording work and storage; tooltips explain the
tradeoffs. Settings apply to new recordings, not sessions already in progress.
Old recordings still play, but missing detail cannot be reconstructed.
Multi-hour Super Detailed recording remains less tested than ordinary sessions;
keep the extra capture optional for long runs.

Existing players can update the ZSG mods in place. ReplayMod remains at
**1.16.1-2.6.27**; importing the pack creates a new instance.
