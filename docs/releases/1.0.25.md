## What's New

- More repeatable Nether natural monster spawning, with stable pack selection and isolated spawn-check rolls.
- Initial fortress protection: eight pack-selection opportunities in the first activated fortress ignore the global mob cap. Normal terrain, collision, and player-distance checks still apply.
- Standardized gravel-to-flint, Unbreaking, and portal wood-lighting rolls through the existing **Standardize Race RNG** rule.
- Customizable match HUD: background opacity, scale, position, player heads, and visible rows. Keep your own row pinned while opponents rotate.
- Reduced spawning and fortress-check overhead, plus spawn-rejection diagnostics behind Seed Debug Logging.
- Expanded game-rule, HUD, and development documentation.

**Important:** Spawning and wood lighting are more repeatable, not guaranteed identical under different conditions. Fortress protection is not a guarantee of eight mobs. The existing 1,300 dragon-tick perch grace period remains unchanged.

All racers should update together. Includes the additions from the 1.0.25 experimental build.
