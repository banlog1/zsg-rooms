## What's New

- Added our own Buried Treasure and Ruined Portal seed banks. Both use our stables requirements. RP seeds require a completable frame without obsidian mining, a golden axe or pickaxe, and usable ignition, without requiring Looting or a fixed stronghold position.
- ZSG Rooms Mode now draws our Temple, Village, Shipwreck and Buried Treasure at 20% each, our Ruined Portal at 15%, and RP Seedbank at 5% for the occasional Looting run. Original filters remain individually selectable.
- Standard ZSG Rooms now enables every room rule except cheats, including reduced zero-cycle fly-aways and shared first Nether entry. The Vanilla Barters variant keeps only boosted barters off as well; Raw is unchanged.
- RP chest and frame repairs are automatic on both portal filters, including random-mode draws. Safe terrain clearance helps expose the chest. The repair setting is now a testing override for other seed types.
- Shipwreck Spawn Close keeps a safe natural spawn within 60 blocks; otherwise it searches for the nearest safe island or coast within 128 blocks, falling back to the original spawn when needed.
- Replay freecam can inspect recorded chest contents and, after the run is released for viewing, predicted unopened vanilla loot where metadata is available. Unknown or changed contents remain marked accordingly. Player sounds are now on by default; seeking stays silent.
- Reduced recorder allocation overhead and increased bounded buffer headroom for chunk-loading bursts, with better diagnostics for incomplete recordings.
- The updater now supports an already-installed Replay Viewer. Added BT/RP overnight collection and faster model-only search policies; the expanded seed bank stays outside the mod download.

Update both **ZSG Rooms 1.0.29** and the optional **ZSG Replay Viewer 0.3.0**. ReplayMod remains **1.16.1-2.6.27**. Older recordings remain usable; chest metadata features need recordings made with the updated recorder. Updaters from 1.0.28 or earlier update only the core, so update the viewer manually once or check again after restarting the new core.
