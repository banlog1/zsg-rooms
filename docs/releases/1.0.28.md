## What's New

- New filter picker with an optional image gallery. ZSG Rooms Mode mixes Temple, Village, Shipwreck and Ruined Portal seeds at 20% each, plus Mapless and Mapless OP at 10% each. New-seed changes reroll the filter; same-seed resets keep it.
- Edit game rules directly from the room. Added seed-type HUD icons and header sizing, optional loading artwork, and presets for the loading progress square.
- Spawn Close now targets a safe location within 48 blocks for land structures and preloads that destination. Shipwreck spawning keeps its previous behavior.
- Improved village smith-loot modeling, including iron pickaxes and diamond alternatives, and temple wood-distance checks. Overnight searches now support checkpoints.
- Replays from discarded seeds are no longer saved by default, with a setting to keep them. Same-seed resets remain continuous; finished races and returning to the room still save.
- Expanded replay viewer: mouse-controlled drone follow, detailed follow with vanilla-style hotbar, health, hunger and inventory, a piglin counter, and customizable dragon/piglin trails.
- Added loading, inventory and crafting indicators alongside pause indicators, with hover explanations. Reduced recorder allocation overhead.
- Optional Quick Mode offers faster scrolling through replays with less detail. Experimental player sounds add footsteps, mining, placements, buckets, eating/drinking, damage and approximate fall impacts. Seeking stays silent. Both options are off by default and do not change recording.

Update both the main mod and the optional **ZSG Replay Viewer 0.2.0** for all replay features. Existing recordings remain usable; inventory/crafting indicators require a recording made with this update. ReplayMod remains **1.16.1-2.6.27**.
