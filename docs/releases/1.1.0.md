## Replay Overhaul

- **New replay controls:** Transparent playback bar, hand-pixelled split icons, a camera selector, overlapping-event chooser, centered progress arrow, go-to-time dialog and explicit hide/show controls. Replay settings are organized into Playback, Analysis and Display tabs.
- **ZSG Replay Library:** Browse, search and play recordings without the old ReplayMod file chooser. Rename recordings without rewriting their contents, or delete a selected recording after confirmation. Choose where new recordings are saved in Rooms' Recording settings; existing files stay where they are.
- **Optional Detailed Capture and Faithful Follow:** New detailed recordings can replay the runner's render distance, F1 visibility, F5 perspective, attack indicator, survival HUD and automatically displayed inventory, crafting-table and chest screens. Use your bound inventory key for separate read-only inspection. Recorded pauses have a gray overlay and centered label.
- **Seeking while viewing containers:** Automatic recorded inventory and container screens work with the normal playback bar, including scrubbing, auto-hide and explicit hide/show. Manual inspection remains a separate view. Run timers and the piglin counter can also be shown in Faithful Follow.
- **Recorded render-distance indicator:** An optional top-left display follows the runner's captured render distance through playback and seeks. Missing coverage is shown as unavailable, not replaced with the viewer's setting.
- **Clickable splits:** Compare recorded milestones across matching race recordings and click a time to seek and follow that runner. Separate reset worlds remain separate; unavailable splits are not guessed.
- **Historical player and mob trails:** Trace players and selected mob types, with customizable duration, color, thickness, opacity and fading. Once the history index is ready, enabling trails or seeking shows the selected recent window immediately rather than waiting for it to accumulate.
- **Piglin analysis:** Fresh recordings capture completed barter outcomes for an item-filterable, sortable history with totals and jumps to each trade. Optional bartering labels and a compact piglin-face counter make recorded activity easier to read.

## Recording Notes

Detailed Capture is **off by default** and applies to the next recording. Existing recordings and Follow: Details remain supported; information that was not recorded cannot be reconstructed. Barter history requires a new recording, but does not require Detailed Capture.

Detailed Capture samples screen state rather than recording video, and does not capture mouse-cursor movement. Recorded render distance cannot recreate chunks absent from the replay. Its bounded disk-backed track reports coverage gaps and stops extra capture at its size limit without stopping normal packet recording. Multi-hour AA testing and comparative performance profiling remain follow-up work; keep Detailed Capture optional for long sessions. Guided tutorials and aligned comparison ghosts are not part of this release.

This release updates **ZSG Rooms to 1.1.0** and **Replay Viewer to 0.6.0**. ReplayMod remains **1.16.1-2.6.27** and is still installed separately. The in-mod updater can update an already-installed viewer. No room-relay or seed-service deployment is required.
