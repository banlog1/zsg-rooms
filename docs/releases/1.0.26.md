## What's New

- Optional automatic replay recording, saved after races or quitting to title. World resets stay in one recording.
- New ZSG Replay Viewer companion: bottom playback controls, auto-hide, five-second skips, player follow modes, and advancement markers.
- Recorded RTA/IGT timers and overhead player-pause icons. Import shared replays and switch players at the same elapsed race time.
- Replay settings, solo replay testing, and a toggle for the recording badge. Recording status stays hidden during playback.
- Standardized opening dragon target-height rolls and an optional Fewer Dragon Fly-Aways rule.
- Race results now compare local elapsed race durations, rather than packet arrival order. Only exactly equal durations draw.

**All racers should update together for compatible finish timing.**
